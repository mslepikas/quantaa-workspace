## 🍷 WINE BASICS 101

### What Wine Is:
- **Grape-based** alcoholic beverage
- **Fermented** (yeast converts sugar to alcohol)
- **Types:** Red, White, Rosé, Sparkling, Dessert

### Main Categories:

**RED WINES:**
- Cabernet Sauvignon (full-bodied, tannic)
- Merlot (smooth, fruit-forward)
- Pinot Noir (light, elegant)
- Syrah/Shiraz (spicy, bold)
- Zinfandel (fruit, high alcohol)

**WHITE WINES:**
- Chardonnay (buttery, oaky)
- Sauvignon Blanc (crisp, acidic)
- Riesling (sweet, aromatic)
- Pinot Grigio (light, clean)
- Chenin Blanc (versatile)

**ROSE WINES:**
- Made from red grapes
- Pink color from skin contact
- Light, refreshing
- Perfect for summer

**SPARKLING WINES:**
- Champagne (France, bubbles)
- Prosecco (Italy, fruity)
- Cava (Spain, traditional)
- Crémant (France, regional)

**DESSERT WINES:**
- Sauternes (sweet, golden)
- Port (fortified, rich)
- Muscat (grapey, sweet)
- Ice wine (frozen grapes)

## 🧬 WINE MAKING PROCESS

### Steps:
1. **Harvest** (grape picking)
2. **Crush** (break skins)
3. **Ferment** (yeast action)
4. **Aging** (barrel/steel)
5. **Bottling** (ready to drink)

### Red vs White Difference:
- **Red:** Crush with skins (extract color)
- **White:** Ferment without skins (stay clear)
- **Rosé:** Brief skin contact (pink)

## 🍽️ PAIRING BASICS

### General Rules:
- **Red meat** → Red wine
- **White fish** → White wine
- **Spicy food** → Off-dry white
- **Dessert** → Sweet wine
- **Cheese** → White or light red

### Specific Pairings:
- Steak → Cabernet Sauvignon
- Chicken → Pinot Noir or Chardonnay
- Salmon → Chardonnay or Sauvignon Blanc
- Seafood → Sauvignon Blanc or Pinot Grigio
- Spicy Asian → Riesling or Off-dry Pinot Noir

## 🍷 READING A WINE LABEL

### What to Look For:
1. **Region** (where grapes grown)
2. **Vintage** (year harvested)
3. **Producer** (winery name)
4. **Grape Variety** (what grapes used)
5. **Appellation** (quality designation)
6. **ABV** (alcohol by volume)

### Quality Clues:
- **Longer label:** Often better quality
- **Specific vineyard:** Premium wines
- **Vintage dates:** Important for certain regions
- **Reserve/Others:** Marketing terms

## 🍷 AT HOME STORAGE

### Ideal Conditions:
- **Temperature:** 55°F (13°C)
- **Humidity:** 50-70%
- **Dark:** Avoid light
- **Still:** No vibrations
- **Level:** Store bottles horizontally

### Refrigerator Storage:
- **White wines:** OK for days
- **Red wines:** Short term only
- **Champagne:** Lie on side
- **Avoid:** Frequent door opening

### Wine Fridge:
- **Dual zone:** Reds + whites
- **Stable temp:** No fluctuations
- **UV protection:** Glass doors
- **Capacity:** 6-20 bottles

## 🍷 WINE OPENING TIPS

### Cork Screw Method:
1. Remove foil
2. Insert screw (avoid bottom)
3. Twist slowly
4. Pull up smoothly

### Wrist Pull:
- Use foil cutter
- Pull cork with hands
- Gentle upward motion

### Sparkling Wines:
- **Hold bottle:** At 45° angle
- **Twist base:** Not the cork
- **Open slowly:** Prevent spray
- **Serve chilled:** 45°F (7°C)

## 🍷 HEALTH CONSIDERATIONS

### Alcohol Facts:
- **Standard drink:** 5 oz wine
- **Alcohol content:** 12-14%
- **Moderation:** 1-2 drinks/day max
- **Empty stomach:** Avoid
- **Mix with:** Water, not sugary drinks

### Health Notes:
- **Moderate:** 1-2 glasses/day
- **Benefits:** Antioxidants
- **Risks:** Addiction, liver
- **Medications:** Check interactions
- **Driving:** Never after drinking
