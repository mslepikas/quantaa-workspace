# Fin Daily Market Report

## Owner
Fin, banking and finance agent

## Goal
Provide Mark with a short, useful daily stock market report.

## Delivery Windows
- Weekdays
- America/New_York
- Morning preview option: roughly 08:30 to 10:00 ET
- End-of-day summary option: after market close, roughly 16:30 to 18:30 ET
- Mark likes the market report as part of an end-of-day summary

## Report Format
Keep it brief and practical. Include:
1. Major index snapshot
   - S&P 500
   - Nasdaq
   - Dow
   - Russell 2000 if relevant
2. Market tone
   - risk-on / risk-off
   - notable momentum or weakness
3. Top drivers
   - Fed/rates
   - inflation/jobs data
   - major earnings
   - geopolitical or macro news moving markets
4. Sectors to watch
   - strongest and weakest sectors
5. Quick watchlist notes
   - any unusually important moves in major stocks or themes
6. One-sentence takeaway
   - what matters most today

## Style
- concise
- plain English
- useful, not verbose
- no fake certainty
- focus on what actually matters

## Preferred Use
- Include this report as the market section inside an end-of-day summary when possible.
- Morning preview can still be used when helpful, but end-of-day wrap is preferred.
