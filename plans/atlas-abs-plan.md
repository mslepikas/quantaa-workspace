# Atlas Abs Plan for Mark

## Goal
Work toward visible abs by steadily lowering body fat while preserving muscle.

## Current Known Baseline
- Age: 62
- Height: 5'8"
- Current weight: 165 lb
- Prior weight: 200 lb (about 6 months ago)
- Walking: 10,000 steps/day
- Training: 3 workouts/week, 20 minutes each
- Uses GLP-1 / Zepbound on a maintenance dose

## Core Strategy
1. Keep daily walking consistent
2. Increase workout quality and gradually extend sessions to 30 to 35 minutes
3. Lift 3 days per week with full-body strength training
4. Add 8 to 10 minutes of core work after each lifting session
5. Keep protein high
6. Lose fat steadily, not aggressively
7. Track weight, waist, photos, and workout performance weekly

## Weekly Training Structure
### 3 strength days per week
Each session:
- Squat or leg press
- Push-up or chest press
- Row
- Shoulder press
- Hinge movement (deadlift variation or glute bridge)
- Plank
- Dead bug or leg raise

### Strength guidelines
- 3 sets per exercise
- 8 to 12 reps
- Progress slowly by adding reps or weight
- Rest enough to keep form solid

### Core finisher, 3 times per week
- Plank, 3 rounds
- Dead bug, 3 rounds
- Crunch or cable crunch, 3 rounds
- Leg raises or knee raises, 3 rounds

## Daily Nutrition Focus
- Protein target: about 130 to 165g/day
- Protein staples: eggs, Greek yogurt, chicken, turkey, fish, cottage cheese, protein shakes
- Protein bars are backup, not the main source
- Aim for a moderate calorie deficit, not an aggressive cut
- Because Zepbound reduces appetite, prioritize protein first so muscle is not lost while leaning out
- Target steady fat loss of about 0.25 to 0.75 lb/week at this stage

## Daily Key Steps
- Hit 10,000+ steps
- Eat high protein
- Stay hydrated
- Follow the day’s workout plan if it is a training day
- Sleep 7 to 8 hours
- Stay consistent, no crash dieting

## Weekly Check-in
Once per week, same morning:
- Body weight
- Waist at navel
- Front and side photo
- Review workout performance
- Review energy level

## Refined Target Notes
- At 5'8" and 165 lb, abs will likely come from getting a bit leaner while preserving muscle, not from crash dieting
- Waist measurement and weekly photos will matter more now than scale weight alone
- A smart next phase is body recomposition: keep protein high, lift harder, and let fat loss continue slowly
